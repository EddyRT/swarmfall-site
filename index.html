SWARMFALL
EXTRACT OR DIE

The city fell. How greedy are you?

Swarmfall is a dark top-down wave-survival shooter about one decision: extract with your loot, or push one more wave and risk it all. Every wave you clear feeds the pot. Every wave you push multiplies it. Die, and you lose almost everything.

  • Zombie hordes flood the ruined streets in relentless packs
  • Bank your credits — or gamble them on a deeper, richer wave
  • Hold the extraction pad while the swarm crashes down
  • Unlock weapons and operatives between runs
  • Short, tense sessions built for mobile
