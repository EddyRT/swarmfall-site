Privacy policy

How Swarmfall handles local progress, advertising data, and player choices.

Applies to Swarmfall for Android Effective July 8, 2026 Contact contact@namyeh.com

This Privacy Policy describes how the mobile game Swarmfall ("the Game"), developed by namyeH Studios ("we", "us"), handles information when you play.

Information we collect

The Game itself does not require an account and does not collect, store, or transmit your name, email address, contacts, or other personal information. Your game progress (credits, unlocks, settings) is stored only on your device and is deleted when you uninstall the Game.

Advertising and third-party data collection

The Game shows advertisements served by Unity LevelPlay (ironSource Ltd., a Unity company) and its partner advertising networks. To select and deliver ads, these third parties may automatically collect certain information from your device, including:

  • Device identifiers (such as the Google Advertising ID)
  • IP address and coarse (city-level) location derived from it
  • Device information (model, operating system, language, screen size)
  • Ad interaction data (which ads were shown, viewed, or tapped)

This data is collected and processed by those third parties under their own privacy policies:

  • Unity / ironSource LevelPlay: unity.com/legal/privacy-policy
  • ironSource Ads privacy notice: ironSource Mobile Privacy Policy

Rewarded ads

Some ads in the Game are optional "rewarded" ads that you choose to watch in exchange for an in-game benefit (for example, reviving after defeat or rerolling upgrade offers). Watching these is always your choice; declining never blocks normal play.

Your choices

  • Reset or delete your Advertising ID: on Android, go to Settings → Privacy → Ads. Deleting the Advertising ID limits personalized advertising.
  • Opt out of personalized ads: use the same Android setting to opt out of ads personalization.
  • Delete local game data: uninstall the Game, or use Android's "Clear storage" for the app.

Children

The Game is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. The Game contains fantasy combat content rated accordingly on Google Play.

Data security and retention

We do not operate servers for the Game and do not retain any player data ourselves. Data collected by advertising partners is retained according to their published policies linked above.

Changes to this policy

If we change how the Game handles data (for example, by adding new features or SDKs), we will update this page and revise the effective date above.

Contact

Questions about this policy: contact@namyeh.com
