Who we are

namyeH Studios is a tiny independent game studio — two cousins making the kind of games we want to play: dark, tense, and built around one good decision worth sweating over.

Our first title, Swarmfall, is a zombie wave-survival extraction shooter for Android. Clear the block, bank the drop, and try not to get greedy. (Everyone gets greedy.)
